Our Story

Meet Coach Carter

Coach Carter's journey is a testament to hard work, faith, and perseverance. Despite never playing organized basketball growing up, he defied the odds by walking on at Saint Francis College — proving that dedication and relentless effort can open any door.

His talent and work ethic took him beyond college basketball, leading to a professional career across multiple countries where he honed his skills and deepened his passion for the game.

He turned down the NBA G League to answer a greater calling — serving the youth of Northeast Arkansas.

Now, through Trinity Sports & Athletics, Coach Carter is committed to developing athletes on and off the court, instilling discipline, confidence, and a mindset for success in every young athlete he trains.
