Section 2

Payment Policy

  • All payments are due in advance of participation.
  • All training plans operate on monthly subscription contracts.
  • Payments are auto-drafted on the 1st or 15th of each month based on your enrollment date.
  • Your first month may be pro-rated depending on your start date. Regular monthly billing begins the following month.
  • Accepted payment methods: debit or credit card only. No cash or check payments.
  • Families with multiple children may request a split-payment plan (half on the 1st, half on the 15th). Split payments are not available for single-child enrollments.
  • EFA (Educational Funds Account) payments are accepted. EFA clients will be scheduled after invoice approval through ClassWallet.

Section 3

Attendance & Session Policy

Group Classes

  • Sessions are held on scheduled days and times only.
  • Missed sessions will not be rescheduled, credited, or carried over into subsequent months.
  • Purchased sessions must be used within the same calendar month in which they are purchased. Unused sessions expire at the end of the month and are forfeited.
Example: If 8 sessions are purchased mid-month and only 3 are attended, the 5 unused sessions expire at month-end and cannot be carried over, refunded, or credited.

Private/Semi-Private Training

  • If a session is missed for any reason, it is the client's responsibility to coordinate a make-up session within the same month.
  • Sessions not made up within the month will not roll over or accrue.
  • Make-up sessions are not guaranteed and are subject to Coach Carter's availability.

Section 4

Refund Policy

  • No refunds will be issued for any reason once a session, package, or monthly subscription has been purchased.
  • Sessions and packages are non-transferable and may not be applied toward other TSA programs.
  • We understand life happens. If extenuating circumstances arise, please contact us directly — we will make every reasonable effort to accommodate make-up opportunities where possible.
  • No refunds will be issued on specials, promotional offers, or discounted training packages.

Section 5

Cancellation Policy

  • A 30-day written notice is required to cancel your subscription.
  • Verbal cancellation notices will not be accepted.
  • Written notice may be submitted via email to your TSA contact or through the cancellation request form on the website.
  • Payments will continue until a valid written notice is received and confirmed.

Section 6

Specials & Promotions

  • All specials, discounts, and promotional rates must be used within the month they are purchased.
  • Unused sessions from promotional packages do not roll over.
  • No refunds on any specials, promotional offers, or discounted packages.
  • Make-up sessions for promotional packages are not guaranteed and are subject to availability.
